Keyboard shortcuts

1–9
Give that stream the sound (mutes the rest)
M
Mute everything / bring the sound back
F
Focus the stream under the mouse (again: back to grid)
G
Switch between Grid and Focus
C
Show or hide chat
R
Reload the stream under the mouse
P
Pop the stream under the mouse out into a mini player
Ctrl+Click
Make a stream the priority: big, with sound and its chat (again: back to grid). On YouTube tiles, Ctrl + click the top bar
← →
Rewind / forward 10 seconds (Shift: 30) on the stream under the mouse
L
Back to live
K
Save a clip of the stream under the mouse
Ctrl+L
Add a stream
Esc
Close menus and cards

Shortcuts work in multi-view while you're not typing.
